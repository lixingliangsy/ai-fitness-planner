Workout blocks and recovery: an ACSM-aligned planner checklist

A usable plan is a sequence of blocks you can actually finish. ACSM guidance for apparently healthy adults emphasizes cardiorespiratory training most weeks, resistance for major muscle groups on 2–3 days/week, and recovery between hard sessions for the same muscles.

1. Define the week before the exercises

Lock training days and session length first. AI Fitness Planner asks for goals, experience, and available time so blocks fit the calendar.

2. Separate stimulus and recovery

Resistance sessions for the same muscle group generally need rest (commonly ≥48 hours in ACSM-style prescriptions). Stacking identical hard sessions daily is a common beginner mistake.

3. Progress one variable at a time

Change load, or reps, or session count — not all three overnight.

4. Treat diet suggestions as optional context

Food guidance in consumer apps is not individualized medical nutrition. Prefer whole-food patterns and professional advice when goals are clinical.
